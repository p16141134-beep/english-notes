Vocabulary for Work Emails

寫英文 Email 最常用到的 8 個單字和開頭結尾句，讓信件更簡潔也更有禮貌。

一句話重點

重點：英文 Email 要短、清楚、有禮貌：第一句說明目的，中間說清楚要對方做什麼，最後用固定句型結尾。

觀念說明

單字詞性中文例句
regardingprep.關於I'm writing regarding the meeting on Friday.
attachv.附加（附件）I've attached the report for your review.
confirmv.確認Could you please confirm the time?
availableadj.有空的Are you available for a quick call tomorrow?
deadlinen.截止期限The deadline for the proposal is next Monday.

例句

I hope this email finds you well.

希望您一切安好。（常見的開頭客套句）

Please let me know if you have any questions.

如果有任何問題，請讓我知道。

I'm looking forward to hearing from you.

期待您的回覆。

Thank you for your prompt reply.

謝謝您迅速的回覆。

常見錯誤

I'm looking forward to hear from you.

I'm looking forward to hearing from you.

look forward to 的 to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing。

We need to discuss about the budget.

We need to discuss the budget.

discuss 是及物動詞，後面直接接受詞，不加 about。

Please reply me as soon as possible.

Please reply to me as soon as possible. / Please get back to me soon.

reply 後面要加 to 才能接人。

小練習

1. I look forward to ___ (meet) you next week.

meeting：look forward to + V-ing。

2. The meeting has been ___ to next Monday because the manager is sick. (延後)

postponed：postpone 延後，這裡是被動語態 has been + p.p.。

3. Are you ___ for a call at 3 p.m.? (有空的)

available：available 形容人有空，或東西可以取得。

補充

注意：email 的結尾敬語常用 Best regards, 或 Best,，換行後再寫名字；不要寫成中文習慣的「敬祝 順心」直譯。
